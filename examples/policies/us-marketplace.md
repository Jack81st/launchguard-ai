## Product claims

Product launch copy must describe only attributes supported by the operator-provided SKU evidence. Absolute performance claims, medical claims, guarantees, and unqualified superlatives require separate substantiation and must not be generated from a supplier description alone.

## Product identity

Every marketplace draft must retain the operator SKU, vendor, target market, and policy evidence identifiers. Titles should name the product clearly without promotional punctuation or keyword repetition.

## Review requirement

A human reviewer must see the proposed title, description, price, FX provenance, contribution-margin floor, compliance findings, and policy citations before any Shopify draft is created.
