## Price floor

The minimum viable price must recover product cost, inbound shipping, duty, fulfillment, platform fees, tax collected in price where applicable, returns reserve, planned advertising cost, and the target contribution margin. A reviewer may raise the price but must not approve a price below this floor.

## Stress scenarios

Every launch review must include a base calculation, a three-percent adverse FX scenario, a two-percentage-point return-rate scenario, and a one-percentage-point platform-fee scenario. Scenario results are decision support, not a forecast.

## FX provenance

FX evidence must record provider, rate date, source URL, currency pair, and whether the value is live, identity, or fallback. A fallback rate requires a visible warning and should be refreshed before a live delivery.
